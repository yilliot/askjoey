- The Edge: "units in Axis Tower start from RM593,600, while those in Brixton and Dover are priced from RM691,000 and RM701,000 respectively."
- 15% Bumiputera discount.
- Prices are per tower, not per layout. A per-unit price list must come from EXSIM or Joey. PropertyGuru's "RM518K – 1.2M" is an agent-listing range below the official minimum, so it is unverified.
- **Restriction in interest (all towers):** "Tanah yang terkandung di dalam hakmilik ini tidak boleh dijual atau dipindahmilik dengan apa cara sekalipun kepada Bukan Warganegara/Syarikat Asing tanpa persetujuan Pihak Berkuasa Negeri." Website English: "The land contained in this title shall not be sold or transferred by any means to a non-citizen or foreign company without the consent of the State Authority." Foreigners can therefore buy, but only with Johor State consent.
- Marketing explicitly targets Singapore commuters and cross-border buyers ("Attracts steady demand from Singapore commuters, expatriates, professionals and short-stay visitors"). EXSIM does not state a foreign-buyer minimum price or quota.
