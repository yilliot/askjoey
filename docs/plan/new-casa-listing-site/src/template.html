Type: why v1 looked stretched, and five pairings in context

v1 set Instrument Serif, a condensed display face with tall, narrow letters, at about 100 px with very tight line spacing. Next to it was Inter Tight, a narrowed Inter. 07 — For Singapore buyers

Questions Joey hears every week

Can Singaporeans buy at New Casa Suites?

Yes, with the Johor State Authority's consent, which is a condition on this title. Foreign buyers also face a minimum purchase price, widely reported as RM1 million for apartments. Joey will tell you which layouts qualify today and take you through the consent process.

What do I pay on top of the price?

Stamp duty, the state levy for foreign buyers and legal fees. Ask Joey for a worked example for the layout you like.

When will it be ready?

Expected completion is 30 November 2029 for blocks C1, C2 and C3. The land is freehold.

Can I rent it out?

Yes. R&F's R&R Sojourn offers property management. R&F calls Princess Cove Malaysia's most-viewed condominium and rental project.

Where can I see it?

At the R&F sales gallery in Johor Bahru (L2-086-A5, Mercu 1) or at the Singapore sales gallery, 60 Paya Lebar Road #13-30, Paya Lebar Square. Joey books both.

Build notes

This is a new project with no existing code to reuse. The repository holds this proposal and the downloaded material.

How it would be built

  • A static site (Astro, one page plus 21 layout links) generated from one content file: facts, layouts, prices, the S$ rate, Joey's contact details.
  • Hero journey: one SVG route map (desktop and portrait versions) and the graded photo; GSAP ScrollTrigger pins the stage and scrubs one timeline; Lenis smooths wheel scrolling on desktop only. Each act is a label on that timeline, so the timing can be tuned without touching the markup.
  • Other motion: reveals, curtains and parallax on the same ScrollTrigger setup; View Transitions to open a floor plan; one pair of easings (silk, glide). All of it is off under reduced motion.
  • Type: Newsreader + Hanken Grotesk, self-hosted, subset to Latin, preloaded (≈ 90 KB).
  • Images: originals in materials/; cropped, graded and output as AVIF/WebP in three widths at build time. The hero stays under 200 KB on phones.
  • Sharing: the WhatsApp/Facebook preview card uses the graded hero with "From RM640,178 · REN 80914".
  • Hosting: a static CDN with Singapore edge locations, deployed from git. Joey owns the domain.
  • Quality bar: Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90; 60 fps through the hero on an iPhone 12-class phone; everything readable at 360 px; AA contrast.

Phases

  1. Joey answers the open decisions and sends her number, portrait, IQI registration number and current price list.
  2. Build the single page, desktop and phone, with all 21 layouts, the contact links and the route hero; preview on the tailnet for El and Joey.
  3. Publish on Joey's domain, set up the share card, a cookie-less counter for WhatsApp taps, and a monthly price-list refresh.
  4. Optional: a second project page (e.g. Exsim Causewayz Square) under a neutral "Joey Lee" frame.
