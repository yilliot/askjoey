Ask Joey · New Casa Suites and Causewayz Square

A hero with more impact

Today's opening spends most of its scroll on a drawn map. The four options below put the photography back in the lead and move the camera through it. All four are built and running in front of the real pages, so you can scroll them before choosing.

Open the hero lab →

Works on the Air and on your phone. Scroll each one slowly, then fast. The dark bar at the top of each page switches between options.

Why today's hero feels flat

  1. For two of its three screens the hero is a diagram: pale lines on ivory, with small pins popping in. The strongest thing we have, R&F's photography, is off stage while the scroll plays.
  2. The motion is lines being drawn rather than a camera moving, so the eye has nothing large to follow.
  3. The award-winning property sites do the opposite. They reveal one full-screen render or aerial through a mask or wipe, set an oversized serif name over it, and let the scroll move through or into the picture. Maps sit one click away. See References.
Recommendation

New Casa Suites: A · The crossing. It keeps the story Singapore buyers care about, that Woodlands is just across the water, but tells it with R&F's own aerial photograph. One frame shows Singapore, the RTS Link viaduct, the Causeway and Princess Cove. The camera then falls from the sky to the front door and ends on the plan. It is the only option that is both cinematic and informative.

Causewayz Square: D · The film. EXSIM's dusk time-lapse is the only moving footage we have, and it suits a full-screen opening. It folds into a frame as EXSIM's distances count in.

B and C are pure brand moments. They are beautiful and quicker to scroll (under two screens), but say nothing about location. Either would also suit the home page later.

The pattern

Almost every property winner reveals one full-screen render or aerial through a mask or wipe, with oversized serif type over it. The motion comes from moving through or into that picture, not from diagrams. Maps sit behind an "Explore the map" button. Most add a branded preloader, which we are skipping.

Build notes

How the lab is built, and what adopting one means

Already done (commit 8897486, live on axon)

  • Each project page is now a template with a hero slot (site/src/components/pages/NcsPage.astro, CwsPage.astro). The live pages are unchanged: their built HTML still carries today's heroes.
  • The four heroes: site/src/components/lab/HeroCrossing.astro, HeroName.astro, HeroWindow.astro, HeroFilm.astro. Each sits in front of the real page at /lab/a/ to /lab/d/. They reuse the site's motion setup (GSAP, smooth scroll) and its facts bar, plan card and captions.
  • Without motion (reduced-motion setting or no JavaScript), every option falls back to a still photo hero with the title and the facts.
  • The film is made reproducibly by bash scripts/grade.sh exsim: graded and looped, no sound, at public/video/causewayz-aerial.mp4.

Adopting the chosen options

  • Swap the hero slot's default in each template. Delete /lab/, and the film too if D isn't chosen.
  • If A wins, today's route hero retires and its map moves to the Location section. The band under the hero changes to a photo other than the aerial.
  • About half a day for both pages, including phone and Safari checks.

Still unverified

  • A real iPhone in Safari (I've only tested in Chrome's engine, at desktop and phone sizes). Most at risk is B's letter mask.
  • Whether R&F has a larger original of the Causeway aerial, and EXSIM a full-resolution master of the film.
  • Low-end Android smoothness for A, which has three photo layers and blur.
